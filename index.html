DPMS

Know what to buy before your shelf runs empty.

Walk your shop, tap what you see, and DPMS tells you what to buy with the cash in your hand. Your order is ready to send on WhatsApp.

No sign-up. Works without data. Your records stay on your phone.

One minute a day is all it takes

  1. Walk the shelf

    One item at a time, one tap each: full, half, low or out. No sales to record and no counting every bag.

  2. Set today's cash

    Tell DPMS what you can spend. It buys first what would run out before delivery, then shares out the rest.

  3. Send the order

    Each supplier gets a clear WhatsApp message. When goods arrive, one tap adds them to your stock.

Built for how Nigerian shops really buy

Prices move. Cash is tight. Suppliers are on WhatsApp. DPMS works with all three.

Remembers every price
See at once when a supplier's price goes up, and who is still selling at the old rate.
Weighs more than price
Ranks suppliers on quality, speed and how often they deliver on time, not only on who is cheapest.
Learns your shop
The more you check, the better it knows how fast each item really moves.
Helps makers too
Bakers and food makers get a daily number for how much to produce, so less goes to waste.
